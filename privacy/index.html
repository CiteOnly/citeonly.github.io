Privacy.

This site collects nothing on its own. What follows is the whole of it.

On this site

No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts. Pages are served as static files. The host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs, which we do not use.

When you contact us

The contact form is a Google Form. What you enter is stored by Google and read by us. Email to sushant@citeonly.com is handled by Google Workspace. We use what you send to reply to you and to set up a pilot if you ask for one.

Pilots

Documents you share for a pilot are covered by the agreement for that pilot, and by nothing on this page.

Who is responsible
