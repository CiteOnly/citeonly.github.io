Research

Research and writing

Three pieces on the same subject: what AI fabrication has already cost, what regulators are going to ask you to prove, and why the common fix does not fully work. Every figure is sourced.

Who these are for Anyone who has to defend an AI-assisted answer to a court, a supervisor or an auditor.

  • What regulators will ask you to prove

    The EU AI Act dates as they now stand after the Digital Omnibus, the US recordkeeping rules that already reach AI-assisted work, and the life-science audit trail requirements that assume every source is attributable. Different regimes, one recurring demand.

  • The trouble with checking the answer afterwards

    Retrieval, free generation, then a verification pass is the arrangement most enterprise guidance now recommends. It reduces fabrication and cannot remove it, and tuning does not change that.

  • What a fabricated citation costs

    Sanctions, refunds and liability findings from the public record: a $110,000 sanction, a consulting refund to a national government, and an airline held to a policy its chatbot invented.

The product these notes are about

CiteOnly answers questions about your own documents using only what those documents say, and reports where each part of an answer came from.
