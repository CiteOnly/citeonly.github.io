About CiteOnly.

CiteOnly answers questions with the exact words of the documents it is given, and the source on every line. 0 factual hallucinations.

Why it exists

AI assistants write well, and sometimes they write things that are not true.

Why that matters here

In most work that is a nuisance. In work that goes in front of a court, an examiner or an inspector, it is a liability, and the usual fix is to have a person re-check everything the assistant wrote.

CiteOnly answers in the words of your documents, each quote with its page. Validating an answer is reading it.

Who builds it

Sushant Daga builds machine-learning systems for work where being wrong has consequences. Before CiteOnly: applied-ML consulting at Biclay Labs, NLP inference and generation at VMock, core ML for document understanding at NanoNets, and electrical engineering at IIT Delhi.
